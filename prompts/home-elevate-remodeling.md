# Elevate Remodeling Demo Site Prompt

Build a premium remodeling contractor website for Elevate Remodeling focused on high-quality quote request conversion.

## Business and audience
- Business type: High-end remodeling contractor
- Audience: Homeowners planning kitchen, bathroom, and full-home remodels
- Core conversion goal: Qualified quote request submissions

## Positioning and tone
- Tone: Upscale, confident, practical, design-forward, trustworthy
- Emphasize craftsmanship, process clarity, and premium results
- Keep messaging clear and conversion-focused

## Design direction
- Elegant visual design with aspirational but credible style
- Strong typography, spacing, and polished layout rhythm
- Premium card and gallery presentation
- Consistent icon usage for services and process steps
- Do not use emojis

## Above-the-fold priorities
- Headline focused on premium remodel outcomes
- Supporting copy for project confidence and process quality
- Primary CTA: Request a Quote
- Secondary CTA: View Projects
- Keep value proposition and CTA visible above the fold

## Required sections
- Hero with quote CTA
- Services for kitchen, bathroom, and full remodels
- Before and after gallery section
- Process and timeline section
- Materials and quality section
- Testimonials and trust section
- Contact and quote form section

## CTA strategy
- Primary CTA: Request a Quote
- Secondary CTA: View Projects
- Repeat CTAs at gallery and process checkpoints
- Encourage low-friction start with short qualification form

## Trust signals
- Project portfolio quality
- Client testimonial highlights
- License and insurance confidence messaging
- Clear process and expectation setting
- Transparent communication claims

## Mobile and UX behavior
- Mobile-first gallery browsing
- Fast quote form access on all viewports
- Easy tap interactions and smooth section flow
- Fast load and clean visual hierarchy

## Accessibility requirements
- Semantic page structure and proper heading order
- Keyboard-accessible gallery and form controls
- Visible focus styles and sufficient contrast
- Clear validation messages and descriptive labels

## Motion and visual continuity
- Tasteful reveal animations for gallery cards and testimonials
- Subtle scroll cue where content extends below fold
- Keep all motion premium and restrained

## Image and placeholder requirements
- Include intentional image slots for hero and project gallery
- Alt text must be descriptive enough for AI image generation or stock sourcing
- Example alt text style: "high-end open-concept kitchen remodel with white oak cabinetry, veined quartz island, brushed brass fixtures, and floor-to-ceiling daylight"

## Form behavior expectations
- Quote form fields: name, email, phone, project type, budget range, timeline, project details
- Form can be frontend-only for now
- Validate required fields
- Show polished loading and success states
- No real backend required for this demo

## Technical expectations
- Use reusable components and maintainable architecture
- Prioritize lead conversion and trust presentation
- Site only needs to appear functional for now
- No production backend required
