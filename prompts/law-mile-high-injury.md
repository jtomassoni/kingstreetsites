# Mile High Injury Law Demo Site Prompt

Build a premium personal injury law firm website for Mile High Injury Law focused on consultation capture and trust.

## Business and audience
- Business type: Personal injury law firm
- Audience: People researching legal representation after an accident
- Core conversion goal: Free consultation submissions and calls

## Positioning and tone
- Tone: Authoritative, credible, premium, empathetic, direct
- Messaging should emphasize client advocacy and clarity
- Support no-win-no-fee style messaging in a professional way

## Design direction
- Premium legal design with high trust visual hierarchy
- Strong contrast, clear spacing, and polished card components
- Use icons for practice areas and process explanation
- Avoid gimmicks and avoid visual clutter
- Do not use emojis

## Above-the-fold priorities
- Headline focused on immediate legal help and consultation CTA
- Supporting text on no-win-no-fee style structure and speed of response
- Primary CTA: Get Free Consultation
- Sticky CTA behavior on mobile encouraged

## Required sections
- Hero with consultation form or CTA cluster
- Practice areas overview
- Why choose us section
- Attorney credibility and credentials section
- Testimonials and outcomes section
- FAQ section
- Contact and consultation capture section

## CTA strategy
- Primary CTA: Free Consultation
- Secondary CTA: Call Now
- Repeat CTAs after major informational sections
- Keep consultation path low friction

## Trust signals
- Attorney bio highlights
- Bar membership and credentials
- Case result style summaries
- Testimonials
- Professional office imagery and clear contact channels

## Mobile and UX behavior
- Mobile-first layout with fast load
- Sticky mobile CTA for consultation
- Simplified forms and clear tap targets
- Phone number always easy to access

## Accessibility requirements
- Proper heading structure and semantic landmarks
- Keyboard accessibility for nav, form, and interactive controls
- Visible focus states and compliant color contrast
- Clear labels and error messaging in forms

## Motion and visual continuity
- Use restrained entrance animations for trust cards and sections
- Include subtle hero scroll cue where content continues below the fold
- Keep all motion smooth and understated

## Image and placeholder requirements
- Use intentional legal-themed image placeholders
- Alt text must be descriptive enough for AI image generation or stock sourcing
- Example alt text style: "confident personal injury attorney in tailored navy suit standing in a modern Denver office with mountain skyline through glass windows"

## Form behavior expectations
- Consultation form should include name, email, phone, case summary
- Form can be frontend-only for now
- Validate required fields
- Show polished loading state and clear success message
- No real backend required for this demo

## Technical expectations
- Clean componentized architecture
- Conversion-focused structure
- Site only needs to appear functional for now
- No production backend required
