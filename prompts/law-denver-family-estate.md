# Denver Family & Estate Law Demo Site Prompt

Build a calm, premium law firm website for Denver Family & Estate Law focused on consultation booking and trust.

## Business and audience
- Business type: Family law and estate planning law firm
- Audience: Families and individuals seeking guidance on wills, trusts, probate, and family law matters
- Core conversion goal: Consultation booking requests

## Positioning and tone
- Tone: Reassuring, credible, professional, compassionate, clear
- Emphasize guidance, clarity, and long-term planning support
- Keep messaging calm and confidence-building

## Design direction
- Elegant, soft, premium layout with strong readability
- Calming color direction with careful contrast
- Polished cards and section rhythm
- Use icons for services and process
- Do not use emojis

## Above-the-fold priorities
- Headline centered on trusted legal guidance for family and estate needs
- Supporting copy reinforcing confidence and simplicity
- Primary CTA: Schedule Consultation
- Secondary CTA: Call Our Office

## Required sections
- Hero with primary consultation CTA
- Services for wills, trusts, probate, and family law
- Process section that reduces uncertainty
- Attorney and team credibility section
- Testimonials and trust statement section
- FAQ section
- Contact and consultation form section

## CTA strategy
- Primary CTA: Schedule Consultation
- Secondary CTA: Call Our Office
- Repeat CTA blocks after service and trust sections
- Keep contact path simple and reassuring

## Trust signals
- Credentials and years of experience
- Professional associations
- Client testimonials
- Clear office and contact information
- Straightforward expectation setting

## Mobile and UX behavior
- Mobile-first reading flow
- Sticky consultation CTA on mobile
- Low-friction contact form and click-to-call
- Balanced whitespace for calm experience on smaller screens

## Accessibility requirements
- Semantic heading hierarchy
- Accessible form labels and input errors
- Visible focus styles
- WCAG-conscious color contrast and readable typography

## Motion and visual continuity
- Use subtle section reveals and gentle transitions
- Include tasteful scroll cue near hero when page continues
- Keep animation restrained and premium

## Image and placeholder requirements
- Place intentional family and estate planning visual placeholders
- Alt text must be descriptive enough for AI image generation or stock sourcing
- Example alt text style: "calm legal consultation scene with a family reviewing estate documents at a bright oak table in a modern Denver office"

## Form behavior expectations
- Consultation form fields: name, email, phone, service interest, short details
- Form can be frontend-only for now
- Validate required fields
- Show polished loading and success states
- No real backend required for this demo

## Technical expectations
- Use reusable components and clean architecture
- Prioritize conversion and trust over content volume
- Site only needs to appear functional for now
- No production backend required
