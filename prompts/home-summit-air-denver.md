# Summit Air Denver Demo Site Prompt

Build a high-converting HVAC service website for Summit Air Denver focused on fast service request lead capture.

## Business and audience
- Business type: HVAC contractor
- Audience: Homeowners and property managers needing repair, installation, and maintenance
- Core conversion goal: Service request submissions and phone calls

## Positioning and tone
- Tone: Fast, trustworthy, practical, local, professional
- Emphasize same-day response and reliability
- Keep messaging action-focused and clear

## Design direction
- Clean, high-trust service-business aesthetic
- Strong hierarchy and readable typography
- Utility-forward layout with premium polish
- Icons for service categories and guarantees
- Do not use emojis

## Above-the-fold priorities
- Immediate same-day HVAC positioning
- Primary CTA: Request Service
- Secondary CTA: Call Now
- Include hero form above the fold on desktop and near top on mobile
- Keep urgency and trust signals visible instantly

## Required sections
- Hero with service request form and call CTA
- Repair, installation, and maintenance services
- Emergency and same-day response section
- Promotions or financing section if useful
- Service area section
- Reviews and trust badges section
- FAQ and contact CTA section

## CTA strategy
- Primary CTA: Request Service
- Secondary CTA: Call Now
- Sticky mobile call/request bar encouraged
- Repeat CTAs after each major service block

## Trust signals
- Licensed and insured messaging
- Response-time expectations
- Ratings and testimonials
- Warranty or workmanship assurances
- Local service area credibility

## Mobile and UX behavior
- Mobile-first lead flow with large tap targets
- Click-to-call controls always accessible
- Minimal friction form with essential fields only
- Fast-loading page and clear section anchors

## Accessibility requirements
- Semantic structure and heading hierarchy
- Keyboard-accessible navigation and forms
- Visible focus states and high contrast
- Accessible labels, validation, and error messaging

## Motion and visual continuity
- Subtle reveal animations for service cards and trust modules
- Tasteful scroll cue near hero to show more content below the fold
- Keep motion smooth and restrained

## Image and placeholder requirements
- Use intentional HVAC imagery placeholders
- Alt text must be descriptive enough for AI image generation or stock sourcing
- Example alt text style: "experienced HVAC technician checking condenser unit at a suburban home during golden hour with clean branded service vehicle nearby"

## Form behavior expectations
- Service request form fields: name, phone, email, service type, urgency, short issue description
- Form can be frontend-only for now
- Validate required fields
- Show loading state and polished success state
- No real backend required for this demo

## Technical expectations
- Keep code modular and reusable
- Optimize for quick lead conversion paths
- Site only needs to appear functional for now
- No production backend required
